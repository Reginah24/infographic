## Numbers we deliberately avoided

The source disagrees with itself on these, so they are left out:

- Under-5 mortality 2025: 32.7 (Table 12) vs 35.7 (Table 10.8) vs 35.73 (Table 3).
- Registered deaths 2022: 25,536 (Table 12) vs 25,567 (Table 11).
- Registered deaths 2025: 39,355 (Table 12) vs 39,378 (Table 10.2). We use 39,355, which matches the fact sheet and NISR's web summary.
- Usable facility cause of death 2024: 64.7% (tables, web summary) vs 65.4% (fact sheet text). We use 64.7%.
